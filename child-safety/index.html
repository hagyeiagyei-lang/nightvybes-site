Child Safety Standards

Last updated October 2026

Night Vybes has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to every video, profile, comment and message on Night Vybes.

Night Vybes is 18+ only

  • You must be at least 18 to create an account. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are removed.
  • Posting is limited to nightlife venues such as bars, nightclubs and lounges, and videos are recorded live in the app at the venue.
  • Filming or posting anyone under 18 is not allowed.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises, abuses or exploits a minor, including images, video, text, drawings or AI-generated material.
  • Grooming, or any attempt to contact, befriend or meet a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, selling or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Sextortion, or threatening anyone using sexual images of a minor.
  • Trafficking or advertising minors for sexual purposes.

How to report

  • In the app: tap Report on any video, profile, comment or message and choose Child safety.
  • By email: support@nightvybes.com, with "Child safety" in the subject line.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.
  • In the US you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

What we do

  1. Remove it. Content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a minor is removed as soon as we become aware of it.
  2. Ban the account. The account responsible is permanently banned straight away, with no strikes first.
  3. Report it. We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement.
  4. Preserve evidence. We keep the reported material securely, as the law requires, for law enforcement, and nowhere else.

Following the law

Night Vybes complies with all applicable child safety laws, including the reporting duties in 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, and with the child safety policies of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Child safety contact

Our designated child safety contact is Harry Agyei, who can speak about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance. Contact: support@nightvybes.com
