Copyright Policy

Last updated October 2026

Night Vybes respects the rights of artists, labels, venues and other creators, and expects everyone who uses Night Vybes to do the same. Videos on Night Vybes are short clips (7 to 15 seconds) recorded live in the app at bars, nightclubs and lounges, and they disappear after 15 hours. Music playing at a venue can sometimes be heard in the background. If you own the rights to something that appears in a video and you want it removed, follow the steps below.

Reporting a video

  • In the app: tap Report on the video and choose Copyright. This is the quickest way for us to find the exact video.
  • By email: send a written notice to our copyright agent (below) with the subject "Copyright".

What a copyright notice must include

Under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512), a notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).
  2. The copyrighted work you believe was infringed (for example, the song title and artist).
  3. The video you want removed, with enough detail for us to find it: the share link, or the username, venue and time it was posted.
  4. Your name, address, phone number and email.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Notices missing these details may not be valid, and we may ask you for more information. Sending a notice that knowingly misrepresents that something is infringing can make you liable for damages.

What happens next

  • We remove or disable access to the video promptly, usually within one business day, and tell the person who posted it.
  • Because videos disappear after 15 hours, a video may already be gone from the app by the time we receive your notice. We still record the notice.

Counter-notices

If your video was removed and you believe that was a mistake (for example, you had permission), you can send a counter-notice to our copyright agent. It must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. The video that was removed (the venue and time it was posted is enough).
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith the video was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, any judicial district in which Night Vybes may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We will send your counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. If they do not tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the video, as long as it has not already reached its 15-hour limit.

Repeat infringers

We keep a record of valid copyright notices. A first valid notice against an account removes the video and gives a warning. Each further valid notice within 12 months earns a strike under our Community Guidelines, and two active strikes permanently ban the account. We may also end the account of anyone who repeatedly infringes others' rights in other ways.
