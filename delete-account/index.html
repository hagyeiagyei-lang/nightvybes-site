Delete your Night Vybes account or data

Last updated October 2026 · Night Vybes, by Harry Agyei

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Night Vybes and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Yes, delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Delete some data and keep your account

  • Videos: Profile → My videos → open a video → Delete. It is removed straight away.
  • Profile photo, name and bio: Profile → Edit profile, then remove or change them and tap Save.
  • Favourite venues: Profile → Settings → Favourites, then remove them.
  • Anything else (for example messages or check-in locations): email support@nightvybes.com from your sign-up email and say what you want deleted. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Without the app

Email support@nightvybes.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, photo and bio
  • Your videos, comments and messages
  • Your follows, blocks, notifications and venue claims
  • Your phone number and check-in locations

What we keep

  • If your account had strikes or a ban: a one-way scrambled code of your email and a summary of the strikes, for up to 3 years, so banned accounts can't simply sign up again.
  • Anything we must keep for an open report, a legal request, or tax records for purchases.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
