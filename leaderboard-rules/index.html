Leaderboard Official Rules

Last updated November 2026

No purchase necessary. Taking part is free. Buying Night Vybes Plus, the DJ plan or anything else does not add points or improve your chances. Apple and Google are not sponsors of the leaderboard and are not involved with it in any way.

1. Who runs it

The Night Vybes leaderboard is run by Harry Agyei, who operates Night Vybes ("we"), 3139 W Holcombe Blvd #A218, Houston, TX 77025, USA.

2. Who can take part

  • Anyone with a Night Vybes account in good standing who is 18 or older and old enough to enter bars and nightclubs where they live.
  • Not open to Night Vybes's operator, team members, or their immediate families and households.
  • Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. When

Each leaderboard runs for one calendar month, from 12:00 AM on the 1st to 11:59 PM on the last day, Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Points reset at the start of each month.

4. How points work

  • 3 points for each night you post at least one video from a venue (a night runs from 6 AM to 6 AM local time).
  • 2 points for each different venue you post from.
  • 1 point for each vybe your videos get from other people whose accounts are at least a day old (at most 50 per video). Vybes you give your own videos don't count.
  • Minus 5 points for each of your videos that is removed for breaking the rules.
  • Ties are broken by more vybes, then by who posted first that month.

Points are counted automatically from videos posted with the in-app camera at the venue. Only videos that follow the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service count.

5. Disqualification

  • Anyone who gets a strike during the month is left off that month's leaderboard.
  • We may disqualify anyone we reasonably believe tried to get points unfairly, for example by faking their location, using more than one account, trading or buying vybes, posting misleading videos or filming people without respect for their privacy.
  • We review the top entries before choosing a winner. If someone is disqualified, the next eligible person moves up.

6. Prizes

  • A month has a prize only if one is announced in the app (on the leaderboard screen) before that month starts. If none is announced, the leaderboard is for fun and bragging rights.
  • When there is a prize, the winner is the eligible person with the most points that month. The prize is what was announced, has no cash alternative unless stated, and cannot be transferred.

7. Winners

  • We contact the winner within 14 days after the month ends, through an in-app notification and message. The winner must reply within 14 days with the details we need to send the prize (such as an email address). If they don't, or they turn out not to be eligible, the prize goes to the next eligible person.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prize. We may ask for tax information where the law requires it.
  • We may share the winner's username on Night Vybes and our social media accounts. Winners can ask us not to.

8. Other things to know

  • Your streak, badges and leaderboard rank can be seen by other people in the app. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep.
  • We may change, pause or end the leaderboard or these rules, but changes won't take away a prize already won.
  • These rules are governed by the laws of the State of Texas, USA, except where the law where you live gives you other rights.
  • Questions: legal@nightvybes.com.
