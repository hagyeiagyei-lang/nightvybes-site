Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

We collect what Night Vybes needs to work and keep people safe. We never sell your personal information, and we never track your location in the background.

Who we share it with

  • Other users see your profile and your posts while they are live. Your exact check-in location is never shown, only the venue.
  • Service providers that run parts of Night Vybes for us, only to do that: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Sightengine and Anthropic (automatic safety checks of a few still frames from each video you post; the frames are deleted after the check unless a serious problem is found), Expo (delivering push notifications), Ticketmaster (event listings, given only an approximate area), Apple and Google (app stores, payments and push notifications). We will list any new provider here before we start using it.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm. Suspected child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • A buyer, if the business is sold, under this same policy.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time, and turn off location access in your phone's settings (you then can't post, but can still browse by choosing a city).
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile, Delete account), which deletes your profile and posts except the safety and legal records above.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California, Texas and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, to object to some uses, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email legal@nightvybes.com and we will reply within 30 days.

International users

Night Vybes is run from the United States, and our providers may store data in the US or other countries. Where the law requires it, we use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for transfers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to people who need it, and admin access requires two-factor sign-in. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires.
